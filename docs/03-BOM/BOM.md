## Overview
The Bill of Materials is the primary organization and record-keeping tool for all components required for the PCB. It contains the price and purchase links for each selected component. To understand my component selection for the PCB, please refer to my [Component Selection](https://mjkim21-dev.github.io/02-Component-Selection/Component-Selection/) page.
