## Lessons Learned and Recommendations
The biggest lesson I learned if you want to be "successful," is to always **ask questions** and **start early**. These two sayings helped me not only in this class, but also as a student as well. Looking forward, if you stay ahead, you will give yourself enough leeway to focus on not only the task to your fullest ability, but also give you more peace. This is important if you want to be an engineer. I figure I'd rather be cool, calm, and collected than just dislike my work. As for asking questions, never be afraid of the bad questions, too; "there are no bad questions". You may get mixed answers, but no worries, just ask the same question a different way until you get the answer you need. It's better to be "annoying" than wrong. 

Overall, I'm probably not the one to ask questions to because I am still a young engineering student, so I have no experience, but one thing I will say is to never let anyone ruin your dreams and to fight with everything you've got to make your dream your reality, as there is no failure, just learning.
